# P1.01 — Add one piece and correct it without AI

**Slice ID and outcome:** P1.01. Locally, with AI paused, a member uploads a shirt and sees one piece in Bower. The piece gets a cutout, and the member edits its category, name and colors. After a reload the piece still has its cutout and edits. A photo with no findable garment keeps a viewable original and stays correctable.

### What each case proves

- **A1 (one piece with its renditions):**
  - A garment upload becomes exactly one active item. It has four current attachments:
    - The original (the upload's asset, 800×600).
    - A 1024×1024 WebP cutout and a 256×256 WebP thumbnail.
    - A PNG mask exactly the original's size.
  - The stage row records `succeeded` with model `isnet_general_use`.
  - The member signs each rendition by its own variant, and the served bytes match the recorded SHA-256. A cutout asset cannot be signed as `original`, and `quarantine` stays unsignable.
  - Brand, price and purchase date stay null, and `category_review_required` is true.
  - `update_item` sets the category, subcategory and name. A fresh read returns them at revision 2, and `field_meta.category` is `{v: 1, source: user, locked: true}`.
  - Neither repeated completion (two new request keys) nor a direct second `create_gather_item` creates a second item or job.
  - A repeated edit key returns the same result, and a changed body under that key conflicts.
- **A2 (honest failure and recovery):**
  - A blank photo's cutout ends `failed/NO_FOREGROUND` with `can_retry`, and only the original is attached. The original stays signable.
  - Manual category and "Use original" save without any AI.
  - Two manual retries rerun the stage; the third returns `RETRY_LIMIT_REACHED`. A stale media revision returns `REVISION_CONFLICT`.
  - pytest proves:
    - Both models find the shirt (IoU above 0.9 with the fixture's pixel truth).
    - The cutout keeps transparent corners, a margin of about 10% and the garment's own color.
    - Blank photos produce `NO_FOREGROUND`, not a "ready" cutout.
    - Corrupt bytes produce `CORRUPT_IMAGE`.
    - Missing or tampered weights are `ModelUnavailable` and are never downloaded.
    - Signed URLs never reach the logs.
- **A3 (ownership and revisions):**
  - B reads zero rows of A's items, attachments and stages. B's `update_item` returns `NOT_FOUND`, B's retry returns 404, and B cannot sign A's cutout.
  - Clients have no insert or update grant on items or attachments (`42501`).
  - The database refuses an attachment that links another owner's asset or item (`23503`).
  - Pending and suspended identities read nothing, and a suspended member cannot edit.
  - Concurrency: a separate connection holds the item row while two edits at revision 1 are in flight. After release, exactly one applies and the other gets `REVISION_CONFLICT` with `current_revision: 2`.
  - The database rejects:
    - An unknown category, or a subcategory from another category.
    - Formality 6.
    - An unnamed color or six colors.
    - An unknown condition tag, or a script-like tag.
    - An eyewear attribute on a non-eyewear piece.
    - A price without a currency, or a future purchase date.
    - Authority fields.
  - A category change prunes subcategory and attributes that no longer apply. Clearing a value is a locked user edit.
- **Stale and forged work:**
  - A cutout completing after the media revision advanced reports `stale`. The current renditions are unchanged, its three outputs are queued as `stale_output`, and the stage shows `canceled/SUPERSEDED`.
  - Bytes uploaded as a 1024 px cutout that are actually the 800×600 original are refused (422) from their measured dimensions, and nothing is published.
- **A4 (emulated device):**
  - The P0.01 zoom method passes: no horizontal scroll at 320 px, or at 200% zoom on a 640 px viewport, for detail and Bower.
  - The Category radio, color swatch and Save work with focus plus Enter. Swatches carry names and a checkmark.
  - The detail page's help text says the original is the privacy-sanitized copy.
  - Axe finds nothing on Gather, detail or Bower.
  - B opening A's piece link sees "Piece not available" and no image.

## Deployment flag and rollback

- The migration is additive. It changes check constraints (asset purposes and roles, job kind and stage) and replaces functions. Old validation jobs keep working.
- **Deployment order:** the Edge capability shape changed. Deploy the worker and the Edge together; a worker with an older contract fails claim-response validation rather than misbehaving.
- **Rollback:** redeploy the previous Edge and worker. Items and renditions stay readable. The database still creates an item when validation completes. An older worker refuses the item-stage claim format, so new cutout stages fail after their attempts, and those pieces stay usable with their originals and manual edits.
- To pause cutouts without losing data, stop dispatch of `item_stage` jobs (the scheduler) or remove the worker's model files. Stages then fail as `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`, stay retryable, and manual editing continues.
