# P1.03 — Gather a mixed batch with care labels

**Slice ID and outcome:** P1.03. Locally, a member can:

- Gather up to 20 source photos in one batch, marking any of them as a care label for a garment in the same batch or for a piece they already own.
- See a corrupt file rejected and an unsent file identified. The successful pieces and their labels are kept, and only the failed or unsent file is retried or chosen again.
- Have a label fill unlocked brand, size and material through the budget-gated gateway. A label never becomes a piece, and removing it deletes its image while the values it filled stay.
- Open a category-first photo guide from Gather, a piece, or More.

**Withheld:** real Gemini label-reading quality and cost (P1.07-T1, needs the paid staging project). Native camera capture and framing outlines are phase 8. The web path uses the file chooser only, so "camera denied" cannot block it.

### What each case proves

- **A1 (20 files, mixed outcomes):**
  - 17 garments and 3 labels are accepted, each label pointing at its garment entry. With a 21st file, the request is refused (422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`) and no batch is created, so nothing is signed.
  - In one batch: a good shirt, its label (sent first), a corrupt file, a label for the corrupt file, and an unsent trousers photo.
    - The shirt becomes the only piece, and its label attaches and is retained.
    - The corrupt file is rejected and the trousers stay `awaiting_upload`.
    - No label becomes a piece.
    - The orphaned label stays temporary. When its window ends it is canceled and its bytes are confirmed absent from storage.
  - Labels must name a garment in the batch or an owned piece. Unnamed, unknown and label-to-label targets are refused, as is a garment with a target (422). B naming A's piece gets 404.
  - E2E: 21 chosen photos keep 20, with "1 photo wasn't added"; the upload button reads "Upload 20 photos".
- **A2 (resume):** the tab closes while the label's upload is held. On reopening the receipt by batch id:
  - The shirt is Ready.
  - The label reads "Not uploaded. Choose this photo again. (care label)".
  - The attention filter shows only that row.
  - Choosing it again adds exactly one job and attaches it to the same piece. The piece count stays 1, so nothing is recharged or recreated.
  - The app says nothing about uploads continuing in the background.
- **A3 (label facts):**
  - A readable label fills brand Uniqlo, size M and material "100% cotton" (`source: label, locked: false`). The provider received the image.
  - A label added to an existing piece keeps the member's brand "Thrifted" and fills only size.
  - An empty reading succeeds with nothing filled, and the piece stays active. A label brand containing a URL fails as `AI_INVALID_OUTPUT` and nothing is written.
  - A vision suggestion never replaces a printed material (pgTAP).
  - Removal:
    - B gets 404.
    - A's removal ends access at once (`not_found` on a grant request).
    - The bytes are confirmed deleted from storage.
    - Brand and size stay.
    - A second removal is 404.
  - E2E: the label attaches, brand reads Uniqlo, and removal with confirmation keeps Uniqlo after a reload.
- **A4 (operability):** keyboard-only file selection and radio choice on a 20-row queue, with no horizontal overflow at phone width. Axe reports no WCAG 2.2 AA violations on Gather (mixed kinds), the receipt, and the guide. The receipt has one `aria-live` region.

## Database, object and ledger evidence

- Label reads use the attempt identity `label_read:{job}:mr{media_revision}:r{manual_retries}:b{budget_resumes}`, with the same budget parking and resume as tags.
- Found during this slice:
  1. **The receipt couldn't load:** once `upload_entries.target_item_id` referenced `items`, the receipt's `items(id)` embed became ambiguous. It now names `items_user_id_source_entry_id_fkey`.
  2. **Receipt order was arbitrary** within a batch (the `clock_timestamp()` fix above). A pgTAP case covers it.

## Accessibility, recovery and zero-AI checks

- Labels are optional. With AI off or the budget at $0, the label stage fails or parks while the garment, its manual fields and the label's attachment stay usable. The label stage uses the same gateway path as tags, which P1.02-A4 proved at $0.
- Removal copy states that the photo is deleted and that brand, size and material already on the piece stay.

## Performance or quality sample and limitations

- No label-reading accuracy was measured: the fake returns fixed text. Real OCR quality on crumpled or foreign-language labels is P1.07-T1.
- Desktop drag-and-drop is not provided. It is optional in the task, and the file chooser is the primary path.

## Deployment flag and rollback

- Labels follow `AI_ENABLED` and the budget, like tags.
- The migration is additive, so rolling back the app and Edge leaves garment uploads working. Labels already attached stay attached, and any label jobs still queued fail after their attempts. Rollback was not rehearsed for this slice.
