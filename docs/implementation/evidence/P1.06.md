# P1.06 — Find, organize, archive and permanently delete pieces

**Slice ID and outcome:** P1.06. Locally, a member can:

- **Find:** search the Bower by name, category synonyms ("shades", "pants"), brand, color, material and tags. Filter by category, color and conditions (OR within a facet, AND across facets). Sort by recently added, category, color or name, and page with stable cursors. The view is kept when opening a piece and coming back.
- **Bulk edit:** select up to 100 pieces with a visible control and change their category or archive them, all or nothing.
- **Archive:** restore from the archived view or the piece page.
- **Delete permanently:** after the consequences are shown, a piece becomes a tombstone. Its images end at once and are proven gone. A storage outage reads as pending, not deleted.

**Withheld:**

- Staging checks: the storage outage was tested locally by stopping the storage gateway.
- A 300-piece check on a real phone. The local check is desktop headless Chromium.
- Wear statistics and wear-based sorts, which arrive in phase 2 with logging.

  - `public.search_items(query, filters, sort, cursor, limit)`:
    - Member-callable, owner-scoped.
    - Validates every facet value, sort and limit (at most 100 per page) and allows at most 8 terms.
    - Returns ordered IDs, total, processing count and an opaque cursor carrying `{sort, last key, last ID}`, compared in byte order (`COLLATE "C"`).
    - A malformed, tampered or other-sort cursor is 422 `INVALID_CURSOR`.
  - `public.bulk_update_items(request, items, operation)`: 1–100 distinct items at their expected revisions; `archive`, `restore` or `category`.
    - A foreign, unknown or deleted ID is 404 and nothing changes.
    - Stale revisions or states are 409 `REVISION_CONFLICT`, listing every stale row.
    - A category change is the member's locked value and resolves review, as `update_item` does.
    - Idempotent by request ID.
  - `public.svc_delete_item`:
    - Revision-checked.
    - Uses `private.discard_item` (from P1.04): jobs fenced, every attachment detached and queued for deletion, vectors and suggestions removed, details scrubbed, tombstone kept.
    - A pending duplicate question about the piece is closed.
  - `public.svc_item_deletion_status` reports `deletion_pending` until every stored image object of the piece is confirmed absent.
  - Deletion reason `item_deleted`.

### What each case proves

- **A1 (search):**
  - On 50 fixture pieces:
    - "Shades" returns exactly the eyewear (including optical frames, through the synonym) and "pants" exactly the bottoms.
    - "navy  shirt" (both terms must match) returns exactly the navy tops.
    - Brand, and a name with its number ("aviators 17"), find exactly their pieces. Nonsense returns nothing.
  - Tops or bottoms, in navy or olive, for hot weather returns exactly the expected IDs and total.
  - Recent order is walked 7 at a time, and name order (by the shared generated names) 9 at a time. Each equals the expected order with nothing skipped or repeated, including rows sharing a timestamp. Category and color orders cover all 50 once each.
  - Garbage, tampered and other-sort cursors are `INVALID_CURSOR`. An unknown sort, facet or category, or a limit of 101, is `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
  - B's search for shades returns B's two pieces and none of A's.
  - E2E:
    - Typing "shades" updates the count and the URL.
    - Bottoms plus navy plus Name sort gives the expected count, and the active chips are shown.
    - Opening a piece and going back keeps the filters and count.
    - "sequins" with 2 filters says so and offers Clear search and filters.
- **A2 (bulk):**
  - Archiving 3 pieces removes them from default results (47 of 50) and shows them in the archived view. `autoSelectable` is false for each.
  - Restore returns the same IDs with the same name, category and colors.
  - A batch including B's piece is `NOT_FOUND`.
  - A batch with two stale revisions is `REVISION_CONFLICT` naming exactly those two, and no piece changed.
  - 101 items, or a repeated ID, is `VALIDATION_FAILED`.
  - A bulk category change leaves each piece `outerwear`, `user`-locked, with review resolved.
  - E2E (`@phone`):
    - Two named pieces are selected with the keyboard (Space) and their category changed.
    - One is archived: 13 of 14 remain.
    - It is restored from the archived view's piece page, with the same ID and name, and no horizontal overflow.
- **A3 (deletion):**
  - B deleting is 404, and a stale revision is 409.
  - With storage stopped:
    - The deletion succeeds.
    - Every image grant (original, cutout, thumbnail, mask) is `not_found` at once.
    - After a cleanup run the status is still `deletion_pending`.
  - The tombstone has no name, category, colors, brand, vectors or suggestions, and the member's list never returns it.
  - A slow worker's later colors result comes back `stale`.
  - A replayed PUT to the original upload slot and a second completion create no piece and revive nothing.
  - After storage returns, the status becomes `deleted`, every image object is confirmed absent, and B cannot read the status.
  - E2E: the dialog lists the consequences and the Archive alternative. The Bower then shows "Piece deleted. Its photos are gone.", and the database holds a scrubbed tombstone.
- **A4 (scale and events):**
  - 300 seeded pieces: first page of 50 tiles in 577 ms from navigation, and "pants" to "60 pieces" in 465 ms (target 1.5 s). Local stack, desktop headless Chromium, placeholder tiles.
  - Captured analytics events include `item_added` and `item_reviewed`, and contain none of: the piece's name, the upload's file name, the piece ID, the email, storage URLs or signatures.
  - The contract rejects names, URLs, IDs and free text in the new events.

## Database, object and ledger evidence

- `delete_item` queues `item_deleted` tasks for every stored object of the piece in the same transaction that ends access.
- The upload key of the piece's original was already emptied at validation. Its post-expiry recheck remains the guard against a replayed upload, and the status does not wait for it.
- No AI calls. Search is a database query, never a model request.

## Accessibility, recovery and zero-AI checks

- Axe reports no WCAG 2.2 AA violations on the filtered Bower. The search placeholder was removed because its contrast failed and the visible label already names the field.
- Selection uses a visible, separate checkbox per tile, operable with Space or Enter, with the count announced. The tile itself still opens the piece.
- **Recovery:** a failed bulk change says nothing was changed and keeps the selection; a failed delete leaves the piece.
- The whole slice works with AI unavailable.

## Performance or quality sample and limitations

- **Timings:** see A4. They were not measured with 300 real images, on a phone or on staging.
- **Thumbnails:** "lazy" here means pages of 50 and batched grant requests, not viewport-based loading.
- **Scroll position:** coming back from a piece keeps filters, sort and search (checked). The browser's scroll position was not asserted.
- **Search matching:** substring matching can over-match short terms (for example, "ring" inside "earrings"). This is acceptable for the MVP by ARCHITECTURE 7.3; trigram or GIN indexes wait for measured need.

## Verification history

- **Label/garment attach race, found by the full E2E run:** the P1.03 label case failed once with the label never attached. When a care label's validation and its garment's validation committed at the same moment, each looked for the other before either had committed, so neither attached the label. The migration now redefines `svc_complete_validation` (unchanged from P1.05 otherwise) so a label's completion locks its parent upload row before looking for the garment's piece. The two completions are serialized, and whichever commits second sees the first.
  - The new integration case "P1.03 concurrency" holds the label's completion open while the garment's runs, then releases it.
  - It fails against the previous function ("expected [] to have a length of 1") and passes with the lock.
- **Earlier tests updated for this slice:**
  - `p0-07-operations.spec.ts`: its event allowlist gains the two new events.
  - `p1-01-pieces.spec.ts`: it now reads the new count status ("1 piece") instead of the old subtitle.
- **Test-only bug:** the integration stale-revision check compared IDs in the wrong order. It now derives the expected stale IDs from the fixture.
- The regression then ran again from a reset database with the totals above.

## Remaining failures, withheld gates and follow-up

- **Withheld:**
  - The staging outage and deletion proof (P1.07 staging pass).
  - The real-device 300-piece and find-a-piece usability task.
- **Claim-issue concurrency (found while writing the race test):** the per-member running limit is checked when a claim is issued, not when it is used. Two claims issued before either is used can both run. This is harmless at the pilot's scale, but it is recorded for the worker-capacity work.
- **Lifecycle registry:** in phase 1, only upload entries and duplicate reviews reference a piece, and both keep only the tombstone's ID. Outfits, wear logs and snapshots (phase 2 onwards) must extend `discard_item` and the deletion proof.
