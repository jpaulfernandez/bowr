# P0.04 — Leave an upload and recover processing after failure

**Slice ID and outcome:** P0.04. After an upload, the tab can close and the validation worker can die mid-stage; on reopening, the receipt shows the original job recovered and published once. Local acceptance passes, including a real `SIGKILL` of a worker process. The staging part of P0.04-A4 (Modal and a deployed host) is **withheld**.

## Commit, build, migration and contract versions

- Code revision: the commit that adds this file on branch `claude/zealous-euler-klyli2`.
- Migration: `supabase/migrations/20260925000400_p0_04_durable_jobs.sql`. It schedules `bowr-job-dispatch` every minute, adds job states, dependencies and payloads, heartbeat/fail/retry/reconcile functions and the `failed` entry state, and hardens publication to the job's own asset key prefix.
- Edge Functions: `internal` (heartbeat, fail), `api` (retry, entry status), `maintenance` (`dispatch_jobs`). A completed or failed attempt immediately dispatches the next runnable job.
- Contracts: `WorkerHeartbeatRequest/Response` and `WorkerFailRequest`, generated into the worker's JSON Schemas. `EntryState` gains `failed`.
- Worker: a `Lease` heartbeat thread (15 s), transient-failure reporting, and abandonment of a lost lease. The dev-only local server can delay each stage (`BOWR_WORKER_STAGE_DELAY_SECONDS`) so the demo can kill a worker mid-stage; `modal_app.py` does not set it.

## Environment, fixtures and clock

- The local stack as in P0.03. Integration tests pause the `bowr-*` cron jobs (`supabase/tests/integration/global-setup.ts`), trigger the scheduler through the maintenance endpoint, and restore the jobs afterwards.
- JOBS fixture: lost wake-up (a job committed without dispatch), worker death (the harness claims and vanishes, and in the demo a real `SIGKILL`), lease expiry, a stale generation, a duplicate wake-up and callback, a canceled target, and a storage outage (`docker stop bowr_storage`).
- Clock control: lease expiry, backoff (`next_run_at`) and stage deadlines (`claimed_at`) are fast-forwarded by SQL, so tests do not wait out the real 120 s lease and 10–20 s backoff. Heartbeat renewal and a real one-second heartbeat interval were observed in the demo.

### What each case proves

- **Demo:** A worker with a 60 s stage delay claims the job and heartbeats (`heartbeat_at > claimed_at`), then its process group is `SIGKILL`ed. A new worker starts. After the lease is fast-forwarded to expiry, the scheduler moves the job to `retry_wait`, then dispatches it. The same job ID succeeds with `attempt_count = 2` and `lease_generation = 2`, and exactly one original object (`…original-g2.webp`) exists.
- **A1:** A job whose wake-up never happened completes after one scheduler run. When the harness worker "dies", the lease expires and the real worker completes generation 2. The predecessor's heartbeat returns `stale`, its late completion returns `stale`, and its output is queued for deletion (`stale_output`).
- **A2:** A duplicate claim token gets 403. A duplicate completion returns `already_applied`, with one published object. Capabilities naming the wrong job or stage, an expired capability, a tampered token and an ordinary user JWT all get 403. A validly signed capability pointing at another asset's key is refused at publication (422, key-prefix check). A wrong lease generation gets 403.
- **A3:** A canceled job stays canceled after its old worker completes and after reconciliation; nothing is attached. Three transient failures end in `failed` job and entry states with `PROCESSING_FAILED`, and no further claim can be issued. The status endpoint exposes only safe fields. A manual retry runs the job with the real worker; a second retry of a ready entry is refused, and the retry limit is enforced. During a storage outage, the attempt becomes `retry_wait` with the entry still `uploaded` (not rejected); after storage returns, attempt 2 publishes. pgTAP confirms one running job per member and two globally, expired leases moving to `retry_wait` and the entry back to `uploaded`, a failure after the third attempt, and a database cap of three attempts.
- **A4:** A second upload hangs as the tab closes. On a new tab (sign-in required, because sessions are tab-scoped), the receipt is reached from Bower: the uploaded photo is ready and the unsent one reads "Not uploaded. Choose this photo again." Three reloads keep one job. Choosing the photo again uploads into the same slot through `renew`, completes it, and creates exactly one job for that entry.
- **Polling (T4):** The receipt polls every 2 s for the first 10 s of activity, then every 5 s until 60 s, then every 10 s. It stops at terminal states, does not poll hidden tabs (`refetchIntervalInBackground: false`) and refetches on focus.

## Measurement and re-estimate

- Local sample (n = 12, alternating HEIC and oriented JPEG, 400×200 synthetic fixtures, one development container): `complete` returned the job identity in a median of 78 ms (maximum 93 ms), and the entry reached ready in a median of 130 ms (maximum 145 ms). This shows the local path has no structural latency problem. It is **not** a representative p95: there is no Modal cold start, no phone network and no 12 MP photos. Remeasure on staging with real phone photos.
- Re-estimate (as the phase plan requests): P0.01–P0.04 are implemented with local proof. Every remaining Phase 0 staging gate needs external accounts (Supabase staging, Google OAuth, SMTP, R2, Modal, paid Gemini), which the original one-week estimate did not include. Plan one to two further working weeks after credentials are available for provisioning and the staging runs, apart from P0.05–P0.07 implementation.

## Accessibility, recovery and zero-AI checks

The receipt's new buttons ("Choose photo N again", "Try photo N again", "Cancel photo N") are labeled and keyboard reachable, and failure text uses the error token with a word description. No AI is involved.

## Deployment flag and rollback

Nothing is deployed. The migration is additive and backfills P0.03 jobs (dedupe suffix and payload). Scheduling is inert without Vault configuration.

## Remaining failures, withheld gates and follow-up

- **Withheld:** P0.04-A4 on staging (Modal proxy-auth dispatch, real cold starts, deployed host).
- **Operational alerting:** stuck work (older than the ten-minute job deadline) is logged by the dispatcher as a structured `stuck_jobs` warning. An independent heartbeat alert is P0.07-T2.
- **Follow-up:** for failed entries, the quarantine object is kept so a manual retry can read it. It has no expiry-driven cleanup yet; P0.07-T2 (expired temporary sessions) must cover it.
