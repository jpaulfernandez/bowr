# P1.04 — Split accessories and resolve probable duplicates

**Slice ID and outcome:** P1.04. Locally, a member can:

- **Group photos:** mark a photo as several small pieces. It creates no piece until the member either keeps it as one set or confirms the parts.
  - Parts are proposed automatically. The member adjusts, removes or adds rectangles with number fields.
  - Each confirmed part gets its own cropped original, and the shared group photo is deleted once every part has one.
- **Exact reuploads:** a repeated photo waits for a choice before a second piece exists: Use existing, Add another, or Decide later.
- **Near matches:** a newer piece that closely resembles an older one of the same category is flagged on its page with the same three choices.

Nothing is merged automatically, and comparisons never leave the member's own wardrobe.

**Withheld:** part proposals come from the pinned local cutout model (connected foreground regions). No AI provider is involved. Their quality on real jewelry photos was not measured (P1.07). Near-match similarity uses the development vector space, which is not semantic, until FashionCLIP passes its gate (P1.07-T2).

### What each case proves

- **A1 (confirmation):**
  - Before confirmation:
    - A grouped photo validates at up to 2048 px and stays temporary.
    - It proposes two parts, one covering the watch and one the bracelet.
    - There are no pieces.
  - Confirming two parts:
    - Creates two pieces with distinct part IDs.
    - A replay with the same key returns the same IDs. A new key gets 409 `ALREADY_CONFIRMED` with the existing IDs. B gets 404.
    - Each part is cropped into its own retained `item_original`, smaller than the source, and its cutout follows.
    - The shared source's bytes are then confirmed deleted.
  - The earrings kept as one set are one piece with no part ID. Two photos made three pieces.
  - E2E: no pieces exist until the member confirms. Keep as one set gives "1 piece added" and Split gives "2 pieces added".
- **A2 (unsafe input):** each of these is refused with 422, with no pieces, no confirmation and the proposals unchanged:
  - A box out of bounds, under 32 px, or negative.
  - Boxes drawn on the rotated dimensions (`ORIENTATION_MISMATCH`).
  - 21 parts, or a repeated part ID.
  - Keep-one with parts, an unknown mode, or an extra box field.
  - Two overlapping boxes become two active pieces and are never compared with each other.
  - E2E: an impossible width is caught with the draft intact. A connection reset keeps the draft, and the resend succeeds with the same idempotency key.
- **A3 (duplicates):**
  - Exact reupload and Use existing:
    - A's exact reupload creates no piece and a pending `hash` review naming A's piece.
    - B uploading the identical bytes gets B's own piece and no review. B reads no reviews through the Data API, and resolving A's review is 404 for B.
    - Use existing is idempotent. No second piece is made, the repeated photo's bytes are deleted, and its care label attaches to the existing piece.
  - Add another and Decide later:
    - Decide later is simply no call. A new Data API session still reads the pending review.
    - Add another creates a distinct piece with its own original asset. A second decision is 409 `ALREADY_DECIDED`.
    - The kept identical piece is not flagged again by vectors.
  - Near matches:
    - A newer variant of the shirt is flagged `vector` with similarity at least 0.92 and stays active.
    - Use existing discards it: a tombstone with no details or vectors, and every attachment's bytes deleted. Keep both leaves it active.
    - Trousers are not flagged.
  - E2E: Decide later survives a reload. Add another and Use existing work, with no horizontal overflow at phone width. The near-match question appears on the new piece, and removal requires confirmation and lands on the kept piece.
- **A4 (cleanup and fencing):**
  - Cancel:
    - Canceling an unconfirmed group deletes its photo (confirmed absent).
    - A later confirmation gets `PARTS_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
  - Removed part:
    - A part is removed while a slow worker holds its crop.
    - The late crop completes as `stale`, and its output is confirmed deleted. The removed part stays a tombstone with nothing attached.
    - Confirming again is refused.
  - Remaining part:
    - The source is deleted once the remaining part has its own original.
    - That original is still granted and fetchable.
- **T4 (AI paused):**
  - With the gateway paused, manual rectangles and keep-one create three pieces.
  - The crops and cutouts succeed, tags do not run, and the provider sees zero generate calls.

## Database, object and ledger evidence

- Crop outputs use the item key scheme `users/{u}/items/{item}/{media_revision}/original-{job}-g{gen}.webp` and are verified at the Edge before attachment.
- Hash comparisons join on `(user_id, sha256)` of the member's own assets only. The review's composite foreign keys reject a cross-owner pair (pgTAP `23503`).
- No AI spend: proposals and crops are local. Tags for parts use the existing gateway path.

## Accessibility, recovery and zero-AI checks

- The split page needs no dragging. Rectangles are number fields labeled per piece. Add and Remove work from the keyboard (E2E). The count is announced politely, and the overlays are decorative, with the list below as the text alternative.
- Axe reports no WCAG 2.2 AA violations on the receipt with two groups, on the split page, on the receipt with the duplicate question, and on the piece page with the near-match question.
- Decide later keeps the question on reopening. Nothing needs AI (the T4 case).

## Performance or quality sample and limitations

- **Proposal quality:** measured only on the two synthetic fixtures (2 of 2 separated). Real photos with glare or touching pieces may merge or split regions, and the member edits them. Proposal time is about 3 s per photo on CPU after the model loads, inside validation.
- **Near-match threshold:** 0.92 cosine (PRD) is applied in the development space, where it catches the same photo with a pixel change. Its meaning for FashionCLIP must be revalidated in P1.07-T2.
- **Harness deletion (A4):** removing a confirmed part used `private.discard_item` through the harness, because the member-facing permanent delete arrives in P1.06. Its fencing and cleanup are what P1.06 builds on.
- A label cannot be attached to a group photo (refused with 422). Labels belong to a single piece.

## Deployment flag and rollback

- There is no flag. Grouped photos and duplicate reviews apply to new uploads only.
- The migration is additive. Rolling back the app and Edge leaves pending reviews and unconfirmed groups; the groups expire with their temporary window. Rollback was not rehearsed.
