# P1.05 — Repair or replace a piece photo reversibly

**Slice ID and outcome:** P1.05. Locally, a member can repair a piece's photo on the web without losing the original or the piece's identity:

- **Fix edges:** Erase or Restore with a brush (three sizes), zoom to 200% or 400%, undo and reset. An area tool does the same without drawing.
- **Use the original photo** instead of the cutout.
- **Try the other pinned cutout model.** This is explicit, and at most three model cutouts are allowed per original.
- **Replace the photo:** a reshoot keeps the same piece.

Each accepted correction advances the piece's media revision. Results computed for an older revision are fenced, and the previous image stays until the new one is ready.

**Withheld:**

- Edge editing on native apps (phase 8). The native build shows the alternatives.
- Testing on real touch hardware. The editor uses pointer events, which cover mouse, pen and touch, but only mouse input was exercised (headless Chromium).

## Environment, fixtures and clock

- Local stack as in P1.04. The storage bucket's CORS rule allows the editor to read its signed mask URL.
- A slow worker is simulated with a harness claim whose lease is then made to lapse (a zombie worker). Per-member concurrency allows one running job, so a replacement can only run once the old lease is gone.

### What each case proves

- **A1 (masks):**
  - Erasing the left 280 px of the current mask:
    - Gives a `manual` cutout at the next media revision. The stored mask equals the edited one byte for byte.
    - The original is the same asset, still attached and granted.
    - Colors are recomputed for the new revision, and the submitted mask's bytes are then deleted.
  - Each of these is refused and leaves the revision unchanged, with no stored edit left behind:
    - A client-made WebP cutout (typed as WebP, or as PNG).
    - A mask of another size.
    - A stale revision (409).
    - Another member (404).
  - pytest proves the composed mask matches the edited mask exactly, and that a mis-sized mask, a WebP or an all-black mask is rejected.
  - E2E:
    - Erase by area from the keyboard, undo, erase again, a pointer stroke at 200%, reset, undo, then save.
    - Afterwards no pixel left of 35% remains and no pixel right of 45% changed.
    - The original is still the same asset.
- **A2 (fencing and conflicts):**
  - A colors job is held by a slow worker whose lease then lapses. The photo is replaced, then the old worker reports red colors, which come back `stale`. Colors stay at the new revision's values and the vectors are unchanged.
  - Two corrections of the same revision sent at once: exactly one 202 and one 409 `REVISION_CONFLICT`. The revision advanced once.
- **A3 (reshoot):**
  - A corrupt replacement is rejected and changes nothing: same revision, original and cutout.
  - A good replacement:
    - Keeps the piece ID, "Work shirt", brand "Kept" (still `user`/locked) and the source entry.
    - Attaches the new original and, once ready, a new cutout.
    - For the old original and old cutout, every storage deletion task is created at or after the detach time, and their bytes are confirmed absent.
  - B cannot replace A's piece (404). A replacement without a target is refused (422).
  - E2E: the reshoot keeps the URL and name, the broken file shows "This file isn't a supported photo." and the piece stays as it was, with one piece in total.
- **A4 (alternatives):**
  - Two alternate-model cutouts succeed (`u2netp`, then `isnet_general_use`). A third is 409 `RETRY_LIMIT_REACHED`, and an unknown model is 422.
  - A photo with no garment fails its cutout once, and no other model is ever tried automatically: exactly one cutout job.
  - E2E (`@phone`): Use original, then Try the other cutout model (`u2netp`), then Replace photo, with no horizontal overflow.

## Database, object and ledger evidence

- A mask is stored as an owner-scoped temporary `item_mask` asset with role `mask`. Its bytes are deleted when its cutout job ends, or at expiry if the job never runs.
- Replaced renditions are detached before deletion is queued, and the pending new cutout never removes the old one early.
- No AI spend. A replacement's tags run through the existing gateway.

## Accessibility, recovery and zero-AI checks

- **No drawing needed:** the area fields and the Erase/Restore area buttons, plus Use original, Try the other cutout model and Replace photo. Tools are radio groups; undo and reset are buttons. Axe reports no WCAG 2.2 AA violations on the editor and the piece page.
- **Recovery:**
  - A failed repair leaves the previous cutout attached.
  - A failed or refused mask leaves the piece unchanged.
  - Retrying a failed `manual` cutout needs the edges to be saved again, because its mask input is deleted when the job ends.

## Performance or quality sample and limitations

- The overlay is recomputed after each operation over the whole mask (about 480,000 pixels at 800×600), with no visible delay in headless Chromium. It was not profiled on phones.
- Only the web editor exists; native gets the alternatives only.

## Deployment flag and rollback

- There is no flag.
- The migration is additive. Rolling back the app and Edge leaves queued `manual` cutouts; they fail when claimed (the old worker does not know `manual`), the previous cutout stays, and mask inputs expire. Rollback was not rehearsed.
